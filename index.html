Privacy Policy

Dieliekevi Tsalida

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Dieliekevi Tsalida is a non-commercial hymn book application developed by VilaKiso. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information Collection

Dieliekevi Tsalida does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information or user data.

The app does not require users to create an account or sign in. It does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location information, device identifiers, or other personal information.

Offline Operation

Dieliekevi Tsalida is designed to operate offline. The hymn content and other resources required by the app are stored on the user's device and do not require an internet connection for normal use.

Locally Stored Data

Certain features, such as bookmarks, recently viewed hymns, and app preferences, may store information locally on the user's device.

This information is used only to provide these features and is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Locally stored information can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

Dieliekevi Tsalida does not use advertising services, analytics services, or tracking technologies to monitor users or their activity.

The app does not sell or share user information with advertisers or other third parties.

Third-Party Services

Dieliekevi Tsalida does not use third-party online services to collect or process personal user information.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Because no personal information is collected by the app, no personal information from children is stored or transmitted by the developer.

Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not maintain a database or external server containing user information.

Any information created by local app features remains on the user's device and can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data-handling practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
